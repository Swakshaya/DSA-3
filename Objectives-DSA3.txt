Objectives:

To automate the allocation of students to laboratory sessions based on their preferences and available lab capacities.

To model students and laboratories as a bipartite graph, where edges represent student preferences.

To apply the Maximum Bipartite Matching algorithm to maximize the number of successful student–lab assignments.

To ensure capacity constraints are respected, with each student assigned to at most one laboratory.

To generate a clear allocation table showing student assignments, lab utilization, and unassigned students.

To visualize the student–lab bipartite graph and matching results for better understanding.

To demonstrate the practical use of graph algorithms in real-world scheduling and resource-allocation problems.
